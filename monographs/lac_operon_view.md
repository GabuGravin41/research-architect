# Lac Operon Regulation Diagram

![Lac Operon — Repressed vs Induced State](C:/Users/User 1/.gemini/antigravity/brain/398f6bda-2945-4869-bbad-e6bbfabadac2/lac_operon_regulation_1786906815973.jpg)

**Left panel — Repressed state (no lactose):**
The lac repressor tetramer bridges operators O1, O2, and O3, looping the DNA and physically blocking RNA polymerase from accessing the promoter P_lac. No mRNA is produced.

**Right panel — Induced state (lactose/IPTG present):**
Allolactose (the true inducer, converted from lactose by a small amount of constitutive β-galactosidase) binds the repressor and causes a conformational change that reduces its DNA affinity ~10,000-fold. The repressor dissociates, RNA polymerase transcribes the polycistronic *lacZYA* mRNA, and three proteins are produced:
- **β-galactosidase** (*lacZ*) — cleaves lactose
- **Lactose permease** (*lacY*) — imports lactose
- **Thiogalactoside transacetylase** (*lacA*) — minor metabolic role
